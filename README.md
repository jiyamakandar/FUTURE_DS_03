
# 📊 Marketing Funnel & Conversion Performance Analysis

## 🚀 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing customer behavior and conversion performance using e-commerce marketing funnel data. The goal was to understand how users move through the funnel stages  from viewing products to completing purchases —and identify major conversion drop-offs, customer trends, and revenue opportunities.

The project was completed as part of the **Data Science & Analytics Internship – Task 03 by Future Interns** using **Python** and **Power BI**.

# 📂 Dataset Information

The dataset contains real-world e-commerce customer interaction data from **October and November 2019**.

### Dataset Included:
- Product Views
- Cart Additions
- Purchases
- Product Categories
- Brand Information
- Customer Sessions
- Revenue Data

### Dataset Summary

- 📌 Total Records Analyzed: **600,000**
- 👀 Total Product Views: **579,788**
- 🛒 Total Cart Events: **8,997**
- 💳 Total Purchases: **11,215**
- 💰 Total Revenue Generated: **$3.38 Million**
- 📈 Average Purchase Value: **$301.76**

---

# 📈 Funnel Analysis

The customer journey was analyzed using the following marketing funnel:

```text
View → Cart → Purchase
```

### Funnel Performance

- **96.63%** users only viewed products
- **1.50%** users added products to cart
- **1.87%** users completed purchases

### Key Finding
A significant number of customers dropped off after the product viewing stage, indicating weak conversion performance and customer engagement challenges.

---

# 🔍 Key Insights

## 1️⃣ High Funnel Drop-Off
Most users remained at the browsing stage and did not proceed to purchase.

---

## 2️⃣ Smartphones Dominated Purchases
The `electronics.smartphone` category contributed nearly **60%** of total purchases, making it the top-performing category.

---

## 3️⃣ Premium Brands Generated Highest Revenue
Brands such as **Apple** and **Samsung** generated the highest sales revenue.

---

## 4️⃣ Strong Revenue Potential
Even with low conversion rates, the business generated high revenue because customers purchased high-value products.

---

## 5️⃣ Customer Behavior Changed Over Time
Purchase activity varied across different dates and months, showing changing customer demand and purchasing patterns.

---

# 💡 Business Recommendations

## ✅ Improve Checkout Experience
Simplify the checkout process to reduce customer abandonment.

---

## ✅ Use Retargeting Campaigns
Target users who viewed products but did not complete purchases.

---

## ✅ Focus on High-Performing Categories
Increase marketing efforts for smartphones and electronics products.

---

## ✅ Promote Premium Brands
Create targeted campaigns and offers for Apple and Samsung products.

---

## ✅ Optimize Product Pages
Improve product descriptions, pricing visibility, customer reviews, and product visuals.
